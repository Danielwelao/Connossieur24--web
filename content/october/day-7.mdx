Not every cyberattack begins with lines of complex code or sophisticated malware. Frequently, the attack targets the human behind the screen.

## What is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is **the use of deception and psychological manipulation to trick people into revealing information, giving access, or taking unsafe actions.**

Instead of spending weeks trying to crack a encrypted firewall, attackers find it much easier to simply convince an authorized user to hand over their access credentials.

## What Are the Odds?

![What Are the Odds? 57% of adults experienced a scam](/october/day-7/2.jpeg)

According to recent global cybersecurity data, **57% of adults worldwide experienced a scam in the past 12 months.**

This statistic underscores a critical reality in modern security: **scams don't just exploit technology. They exploit people.**

## The Target is the Person

![The Target is the Person - Emotional Triggers](/october/day-7/3.jpeg)

The fundamental goal of a social engineering attack is simple: **get you to act before you stop to think.**

To achieve this, attackers manipulate core human emotions and instinctual responses:

* **Urgency (`"Act now! Offer expires!"`)**: Creates artificial time pressure to rush your decision making before you can double-check the facts.
* **Fear (`"Your account is locked."`)**: Induces immediate panic, prompting you to click fraudulent links in an effort to restore access.
* **Trust (`"Hey, it's me. I need a favor."`)**: Exploits personal or professional relationships by impersonating co-workers, friends, or trusted contacts.
* **Authority (`"This is your bank calling."`)**: Leverages official stature, corporate hierarchy, or law enforcement personas to demand compliance without question.

## Key Takeaway

Technical controls like firewalls and antivirus software are essential, but your awareness is the final line of defense. Whenever an incoming message triggers a strong emotional response—whether panic, pressure, or obligation—pause and verify the sender independently before taking any action.
